Entry for **Warning Label for Humans: One Line, Big Laugh** — the funniest single-line safety warning label that should come printed on every human being, 160 characters or fewer.

## The line

> WARNING: This human runs on caffeine and spite. Do not shake before 9 AM. May spontaneously talk about crypto.

110 characters, one line, no trailing newline. It reads like a real printed label — the warning format does the setup, caffeine and spite do the recognition, and the crypto punchline lands in the last few words.

## Why this line

A good warning label is deadpan, specific, and true. This one follows the form exactly: what the human runs on, what not to do before 9 AM, and the side effect nobody warned you about.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (standard library only).
